ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Made for the back room, not the boardroom.

Running a food stall usually means notebooks, guesswork, and counting leftovers at night. Stockroom replaces that with one calm place: the owner sets up the items and prices, staff submit what they took and returned, and the numbers do themselves. It works on any phone browser, and staff don’t even need an account, just a code from you.

WHAT YOU GET

Everything you need to stay on top of stock.

▦

Live stock count

Stock drops automatically as items are sold, so you always know what’s left on the shelf.

₱

Sales worked out for you

Staff enter what they took and what came back. Stockroom computes units sold and earnings.

⌗

Staff access codes

Give your team your own code. Whatever they submit goes only into your shop’s records.

☷

Daily reports, saved automatically

Each day is archived on its own. Browse past days by date and print a report any time.

↯

Works with weak signal

If a phone loses connection mid-submission, it’s saved on the device and sent once it’s back.

◈

Private by default

Every owner has their own foods, stock, submissions and reports. Nobody else can see them.

HOW IT WORKS

Up and running in three steps.

  1. 1

    Create your shop account

    Sign up with Google or your email. You get your own staff code right away.

  2. 2

    Add your items

    List your foods and drinks with prices and starting stock. Share your staff code with your team.

  3. 3

    Staff submit, you see results

    Staff log in with the code and record what they took and returned. Your dashboard updates with sales and stock.
